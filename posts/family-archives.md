A lot of people have a folder on a hard drive somewhere called *"Old videos"*, a thousand clips from phones, weddings, holidays, kids' birthdays. Most of them never get watched again. Not because anyone stopped caring, but because finding the specific moment you want, *"the part where mom laughed so hard she cried"*, means watching dozens of clips until you stumble on it.

Scrubless is for that folder.

## What people use it for

Some examples that have come up:

- *"the part of the wedding where dad's speech got serious"*
- *"when she said her first words"*
- *"the moment we lit the candles on the cake"*
- *"the toast at thanksgiving"*
- *"when the dog came running"*

You don't need to remember which video it's in. You drop the whole archive, type what you remember, click the moment.

## How it deals with old, messy footage

A lot of personal archives are formatted oddly. Old camcorder rips. Phone clips in `.mov`. Slow-motion clips with no audio. Vertical recordings someone never rotated. Scrubless reads any of `.mp4`, `.mov`, `.mkv`, `.webm`, `.m4v` and indexes them the same way.

When there's no audio (a lot of phone clips are recorded muted by accident), it just searches by what's in the frames. *"the part where she's wearing the yellow dress"* still works without any spoken cue.

## The "I forgot this existed" feeling

The thing people don't expect is how much they find that they'd forgotten. The way Scrubless surfaces moments isn't *"the file you searched for"*, it's *"every moment matching what you described."* So a search for *"singing happy birthday"* turns up every clip from every birthday across years, not just the one you were looking for.

This is the unexpected use case: not "find this specific clip" but "show me every time we did this."

## Honest notes

A few things worth knowing:

- **Indexing takes time.** Each video has to get scanned once. A 10-minute clip takes maybe a minute or two. A long archive on the first pass can take an evening. It's a one-time cost, after that, search is instant.
- **Old phone audio can be rough.** Transcription accuracy depends on how loud the spoken bit was relative to background noise. The visual search still helps.
- **Your videos stay yours.** They go on Scrubless's storage but they're only accessible to you (or whoever you share the link with). Anonymous uploads auto-expire.

If you've got a folder of old family videos sitting unsearched, try it on one of them at [getscrubless.com](/). It's surprisingly emotional the first time you find a clip you'd half-forgotten.
