Most teams have years of meeting recordings sitting in a Drive folder no one opens. They were a good idea on the way in ("we'll have a record of every all-hands!") and a brick on the way out ("…in which file?").

Scrubless is what makes those recordings useful again. Drop the folder in, and you can search every meeting in plain English.

## What teams actually search for

In the few months people have been using this, three patterns come up over and over:

**Decisions.** *"Where did we decide to move the launch to Q3?"*, the kind of question that surfaces six months later when someone asks why and no one remembers.

**Quotes.** *"Did the CEO say anything about hiring on the last all-hands?"*, useful for HR / comms / internal docs that need a primary source.

**Action items.** *"Who said they'd handle the contract?"*, useful when the meeting notes are thin and the actual commitment is buried in audio.

For each one, the workflow is the same: type the query, click the moment, watch ten seconds, copy the quote.

## Why search-by-description works well on meetings

Meetings are mostly speech, so the transcription does the heavy lifting. But Scrubless will also catch visual cues if you screen-share, *"when we pulled up the org chart"* or *"the slide about runway"* will land on the right frame even if no one read it aloud.

A few queries that have worked on real archives:

- *"someone says we should hire"*
- *"the part where the room got quiet"*
- *"when Sarah agreed to own the launch"*
- *"the slide about Q3 numbers"*

## The Ask feature for cross-meeting questions

Library mode is when this gets really useful for a team. Once a folder of meetings is indexed, the Ask tab will give you a written answer with citations to specific clips.

> *"Has anyone mentioned a budget freeze on the last six all-hands?"*

You get back: *"Yes, discussed briefly on [2026-04-12 @ 14:22] and again in detail on [2026-05-30 @ 23:01]."* The bracketed citations are clickable; you jump to the moment.

This is the feature that converts skeptical teams. The first time it pulls a half-remembered quote out of 30 hours of meetings, the case is made.

## What it isn't

A meeting-summarizer SaaS. There are good ones if all you want is "give me bullet points for every meeting." Scrubless is for the harder problem: *"the answer is somewhere in 80 hours of recordings."*

Also: it doesn't ingest from Zoom / Meet / Teams APIs. You drop in the recorded files yourself (or the folder you sync them to). That's a feature, not a bug, your meetings stay on your storage, not in a third-party transcription queue.
